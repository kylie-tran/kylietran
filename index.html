About Me

Hello! I'm Huong Tra, but you can call me Kylie! 🌟 I'm currently studying Business Administration at Yuan Ze University as an exchange student from Banking University of Ho Chi Minh City.

I'm passionate about sustainable finance and responsible investing, believing that financial decisions can drive positive environmental and social change. My goal is to bridge the gap between traditional finance and sustainability practices.

My focus areas include ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) investing, green bonds, impact investing, and sustainable development finance. I'm particularly interested in how financial institutions can integrate sustainability principles into their investment strategies and operations.

Through my coursework and personal projects, I've developed strong analytical skills and technical proficiency in tools like Power BI, Excel, SAP, and Python. I'm eager to contribute to organizations that prioritize both profitability and planet-positive impact! 🌍
